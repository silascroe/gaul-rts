# GaulRTS prototype art

This is the first readable art pass for the Broken Oath battle. The assets are intentionally provisional: they exist to make the battlefield legible while the rules and camera are still changing.

Terrain textures are 128×128 and are drawn as repeated textures. Object sprites use transparent PNG backgrounds. Unit and hillfort artwork is faction-neutral; ownership remains visible through the colored rings and underlays drawn by `src/main.gd`.

The assets were generated as rough visual placeholders and should be replaced or refined after a few real play sessions. Do not treat the current proportions, palette, or silhouettes as locked art direction.
