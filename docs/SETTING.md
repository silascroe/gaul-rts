# The Broken Oath

## Premise

The Sable Vale is a rich border valley in an alternate Iron Age Gaul. The Alder League and the Flint Crown have shared its crossings, groves, and iron workings for generations. A broken marriage oath has turned an old rivalry into open war.

The game begins at the scale an RTS can make legible: one hillfort, a handful of retainers, nearby resources, and a rival whose first move is already under way. The wider world can become stranger later.

## Tone

Grounded, archaic, political, and a little uncanny. The setting should feel like a place with customs and obligations rather than a fantasy theme park. Omens, sacred places, and ritual politics are available; literal supernatural powers are not assumed.

## Guardrails

- This is inspired by pre-Roman Gaul, not a claim to recreate it precisely.
- The factions are invented instead of flattening real peoples into game stereotypes.
- Material culture should carry the period: timber, earthworks, iron, woven goods, animals, roads, rivers, and hillforts.
- The alternate history needs a clear point of divergence once the campaign expands beyond this first scenario.
- The game should stay readable at old-school RTS scale. If a detail cannot be understood while the battle is moving, it belongs in flavor text or a later layer.

## Current scenario

**The Alder League** begins on the western side of the Sable Vale. Its immediate advantage is organization and access to the river road.

**The Flint Crown** holds the eastern hillfort. Its first soldiers are already moving, and its workers will build the resources needed to keep the pressure on.

The current objective is intentionally blunt: break the enemy hillfort. Later scenarios can make victory political, economic, religious, or territorial instead of purely military.
